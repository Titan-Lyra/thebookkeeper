Surprise! Thought I was dead, did you? You were right, of course — I *did* die fighting a hag with a cursed artifact lodged inside me. Congratulations for keeping up. Gold star, et cetera. I’m indeed very dead, and no, I won’t be making a miraculous return this lifetime. Team 14 will have to carry on without me.  

Still… someone has to chronicle their misadventures, and who better than yours truly? I’m *obligated* to record every tale, even posthumously. Since I already know how this story ends, I’ll try to be a little kinder this time. No spoilers, though — I’ll pretend ignorance. (But between us, [Caelum] dies peacefully on a fishing trip. Or does he?)

### Grey Ghost Tavern

Our story begins in the *Grey Ghost Tavern*, where Team 14 enjoyed a well-earned breakfast. Had they not been responsible for my untimely demise, I might have even paid the bill. Alas.  

My old friend [Varrin Axebreaker] of the Axebreaker clan entered the tavern. The dwarf instantly recognized the group I’d told him about. He explained that the list of the Overking’s children — the one everyone’s been after — was locked away in a safe once owned by our former ally [Korda Glintstone], now known as *Prisoner 13*.  

After nearly twenty years, the clan had finally found the safe — but it wasn’t protected by an ordinary lock. It was sealed by magic. Worse still, much of the Axebreaker clan’s gold had been stolen and hidden inside. Varrin needed the key, and Team 14 needed what was inside. He offered them a modest share — *2% of the contents* — their best potential reward yet.  

Unfortunately, when Korda was captured years ago, she refused to give up the safe’s secrets. Since then, she’s been rotting in *Revel’s End*, a remote prison at the island of Admunfort. To open the safe, the group would have to infiltrate the prison and retrieve the key — by any means necessary (though, preferably, not too indecent).

Varrin had arranged for his agents to intercept new recruits bound for the prison — guards, cooks, and staff. There was one problem: [Grof] and [Alice]. Their bright red and silver skin made them a little too memorable.  

To solve this, Grof disguised himself as a last-minute inmate named [Karn Orcbane], a notoriously violent orcish murderer still “at large.” Alice, unwilling to play prisoner, hid in a barrel aboard the ship *Jolly Pelican*, smuggled aboard by [Rowan], posing as a cheff named *Varron Holt*.  

Meanwhile, [Caelum] and [Amber] took the roles of guards — *Tomas Redwell* and *Penny Tallowmere* respectively.


### The Jolly Pelican

Aboard the *Jolly Pelican*, the crew successfully blended in. Prisoners in the back, guards in the middle, staff up front. There were twelve inmates — until Grof’s new alias brought the total to thirteen.  

Head guard [Branik Thalor] briefed the newcomers: “Never use names. Only ‘yes, sir’ and ‘no, sir.’” Simple enough, though perhaps a bit ominous.  

Among the prisoners were two familiar faces — former curator [Alda Arkin] and her guard [Garent Millaneff], both convicted of fraud. Unless Team 14 makes a *very bad decision*, they’ll stay right where they belong.  

After half a day of rough seas, the ship reached the frozen island of Admunfort — home of the infamous *Revel’s End*.


### Revel’s End

Upon arrival, the new guards were assigned to the barracks, the cooks to the kitchen, and the prisoners to their cells. Alice, still inside her barrel, was promptly sent to the kitchen as well. Rowan decided to let her rest a little longer before setting her free — probably out of laziness more than kindness.  

Grof was placed in a cell with a halfling woman who seemed friendly enough. She claimed her actions had killed bad people — and some good ones, too. (I don’t buy it, but that’s what she insists.)  

The next morning, the guards began their first shifts. Amber and Caelum were sent on patrol while Rowan prepared breakfast. Alice stayed hidden. Grof, meanwhile, was summoned by Warden [Marta Marthannis], who treated her with unsettling warmth — a trick she apparently uses to intimidate new inmates. Whether it works is anyone’s guess.  

Afterward, Amber and Caelum were ordered to escort the “orc” back to her cell. Naturally, they took a detour to speak with Rowan (and the still-concealed Alice). After a short discussion, they agreed: return Grof to his cell, then find *Prisoner 13*.  

What could possibly go wrong?

#### “Mommy!”

“Amber?” a familiar voice called out.  
I knew this might happen — I’d calculated an 85% chance, which was good enough for me.  

After years apart, Amber finally found her mother, [Arielle Kervel], imprisoned in Revel’s End. Their reunion was brief but heartfelt. Let’s just hope Team 14 doesn’t get any ideas about breaking her out.

#### Prisoner 13

A still-shaken Amber (this is becoming a pattern) joined Caelum — with Alice lurking in the shadows — to meet the stoic *Prisoner 13* herself. At first, Korda refused to say anything. Only after the group admitted their purpose did the tattooed dwarf show interest.  

A deal was struck: in exchange for the Warden’s ledger — containing every prisoner’s information — she would give them the key.
