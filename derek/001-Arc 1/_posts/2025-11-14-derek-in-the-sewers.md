### Returning to the Observatory
With four diary pieces, we knew we had to return to find the last one. I suggested to just forge all the pieces and give it to [Edmond Harrington]. Lou dislikes the man, so we could catch two birds with one stone. Lou was also so kind to give some blueprints to the Taxabi.

#### Lou's backstory
Once upon a time there was a boy named Lucian, later to be known as the legendary Lou.
His father wasn't a kind man, so he told. A man named John.
How he wished his boy had divine powers, but alas, none was given.

Night and days he spends in a crypt. Surrounded by the dead.
His fear became comfort, when light was not near.
Sol appeared before the boy and said "you shall be my herald".
And the divine powers were received.

John was proud and finally gave his blessing.
The boy's heart was broken, nevertheless.

### Leo attacks and dies
When we returned to the observatory we suddenly were attacked. A big crowd had gathered around the building. While the others tried to look for the missing diary piece, I decided to do some star gazing.

A window was shattered and people dressed in white entered the room. Their leader named [Leo], follower of the god Tyr, rightfully blamed Edmond for the mess they created. However, he shouldn't have done it this aggressively. We decided to distract the attackers while leading [Jonathan], [Abigail] and Edmond to the secret tunnels. I tried to talk with the big man (Leo, not Lou), but he decided to just attack. Before I knew it I was being hold by some unseen force. Of course, it wouldn't take long before I broke free. Because of this Leo was allowed to die.

Lou rushed to came to my aid. Thank you for that by the way. Ash went for the daring move to splash acid, which also hit me. As I stated at the start of this log, I don't hold a grudge to the man. It was effective though. The battle was won, and we could safely escort the Harrington's. They were heading towards the Academy to get to safety.

It was time for use to get some well-earned rest.
